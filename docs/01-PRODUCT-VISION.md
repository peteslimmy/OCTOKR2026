# 4CORE-OKR Product Vision

## Product vision

4CORE-OKR is the organisation's system of record for strategic execution, weekly BU accountability, governance, performance visibility, and controlled violation-fund administration.

The product replaces spreadsheet-based execution reporting with an auditable digital operating model.

## Core problem

Before 4CORE-OKR, strategic and weekly execution reporting was largely spreadsheet-based. This created:

- weak strategic execution;
- limited accountability;
- inconsistent weekly reporting;
- manual reconciliation;
- unreliable management visibility;
- weak performance measurement;
- governance/compliance gaps;
- financial consequences that lacked a central auditable workflow.

## Product outcome

Management should be able to answer, without asking someone to reconcile spreadsheets:

1. What is the organisation trying to achieve this quarter?
2. What are the KRs and Sub-KRs?
3. What did each BU commit to this week?
4. Which goals were mapped to which Sub-KRs?
5. What was completed?
6. What is each BU's weekly execution score?
7. Which BUs failed to report?
8. Were any integrity corrections made?
9. What penalties were applied or waived?
10. What is the current violation-fund balance?
11. What disbursements were requested and approved/rejected?
12. Can every important number be traced back to a source event?

## Product principles

### Strategy is shared

Annual Theme, Quarterly Objective, KRs and Sub-KRs are organisation-wide.

### Execution is BU-owned

Weekly reports are owned by the BU. The BU Lead is the authorised operator.

### Time is a control

Tuesday 18:00 and the following Tuesday 11:00 are hard system boundaries.

### Numbers must be explainable

Scores, penalties, balances, and compliance statuses must be derived from auditable facts.

### Administrators remain accountable

Higher permissions do not create an audit bypass.

### Financial actions are controlled

HR requests. Finance decides. The ledger records the result.

## Primary personas

### BU Lead

Needs to define the BU commitment, monitor execution, and finish the reporting cycle on time.

### Presenter

Needs a presentation-optimised view of every BU and the ability to perform governed task corrections during presentation.

### Executive/Director

Needs reliable organisation-wide visibility without operational editing controls.

### HR

Needs attendance, violation, quote, and financial-fund workflows.

### Finance

Needs a controlled queue for reviewing and deciding on disbursement requests.

### Admin/SuperAdmin

Needs system governance, strategy administration, security, configuration, audit, and operational support.

## Non-goals

4CORE-OKR does not currently aim to be:

- a payroll system;
- a multi-currency finance platform;
- a multi-organisation SaaS product;
- a full HRIS;
- an ERP;
- an autonomous business-outcome measurement engine.
